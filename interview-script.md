# LessonLock — Teacher Interview Script (1 page)

**Goal:** Validate demand for no-show protection among online music teachers. Target: 30 interviews. Kill-gate pre-check: if <50% say they'd require card-on-file → revisit positioning.

---

## Recruitment channels
- "Piano Teachers" Facebook Group (150k+)
- "Guitar Teachers Forum"
- r/piano, r/Guitar, r/Teachers
- Teacher Discords / music teacher communities
- LinkedIn (search "piano teacher" / "guitar teacher")

**Outreach message (DM or group post):**
> Hi! I'm researching how online music teachers handle no-shows and late cancels. Would you be open to a 15-min chat? No pitch — just learning. Happy to share what other teachers are doing. [Calendly link]

---

## Interview script (15 min)

### Intro (1 min)
"Thanks for chatting. I'm exploring a problem many teachers mention — no-shows and late cancels. No product pitch today, just learning from you. Mind if I take notes?"

### Section A — Current situation (4 min)
1. How many students do you teach, and what's your rate?
2. What % of lessons get cancelled late or no-show in a typical month?
3. How do you currently handle late cancels / no-shows? (policy, charges, chasing)
4. How much time per week do you spend chasing payments or rebooking?
5. Have you ever lost money to a no-show? Roughly how much?

### Section B — Tools & pain (4 min)
6. What tools do you use for booking/scheduling/payments? (Calendly, Stripe, Venmo, PayPal, etc.)
7. What's the most frustrating part of your current setup?
8. Have you tried requiring a card on file or a deposit? What happened?
9. If you could wave a magic wand, what would change about how you handle no-shows?

### Section C — Card-on-file reaction (4 min)
10. "What if students put a card on file once when booking, and you're automatically paid a kept fee (e.g. 50% of the lesson) if they late-cancel or no-show — with a 1-click refund if you want to forgive it?"
    - Reaction (1-5 scale): Would you use this?
    - What concerns would you have? (parent pushback, trust, fees)
    - What would make you NOT use it?
11. Would you require card-on-file for all new students, or only after a no-show?
12. What's a fair kept-fee % and cancel window for your studio?

### Section D — Pricing & close (2 min)
13. If this existed today, what would you pay monthly? ($12 / $29 / other)
14. Would you want SMS reminders to students, or email is enough?
15. Can I follow up in a few weeks when I have something to show? (get email)

## Kill-gate pre-check (after 10 interviews)
- If <50% say they'd require card-on-file → revisit positioning before continuing.
- Track: interviews done, avg pain score, avg card-on-file reaction, % willing to pay.

## Notes
- Don't pitch during the interview — listen. Pitch only at the end if they ask.
- Record exact quotes for testimonials ("I recovered $X").
- Ask for permission to follow up.
- Target 30 interviews in 14 days (~2/day).
