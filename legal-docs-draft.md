## 1. Who we are

**Student data (collected on behalf of Tutor):**

- Provide and operate the Service
- Process payments via Stripe (on Tutor's connected account)
- Send transactional emails (booking confirmations, charge receipts, reminders)

- Improve the Service (analytics, debugging)
- Comply with legal obligations

## 4. Data sharing
We share data with:
- **Stripe** — payment processing (Tutor's connected account)
- **Supabase** — database and authentication hosting

- **Twilio** — SMS delivery (Pro tier)
- **Vercel** — hosting

We do NOT sell personal data. We do NOT share data with advertisers.

## 5. Data retention
- Tutor account data: retained while account is active; deleted within 30 days of account closure.
- Student/lesson data: retained as long as the Tutor's account is active; deleted with the account.
- Charge/evidence logs: retained for 7 years for dispute/audit purposes (legal requirement).
- Backups: deleted within 90 days of primary deletion.
