# LessonLock — 10 Policy Templates (ready to use)

Each template = policy window + kept fee % + ready-to-send parent message. Tutors pick one and customize.

---

## 1. Standard (24h / 50% kept) — RECOMMENDED DEFAULT
**Policy:** Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the lesson. 50% of the lesson fee charged for late cancels or no-shows.
**Parent message:**
> Hi! To reserve your lesson slot, I require a card on file. Cancellations made 24+ hours ahead are free. Late cancels or no-shows are charged 50% of the lesson fee. This keeps scheduling fair for everyone. Thanks!

## 2. Strict (48h / 100% kept)
**Policy:** Free cancellation up to 48 hours before. Full lesson fee charged for late cancels or no-shows.
**Parent message:**
> To hold your lesson slot, a card is required on file. Cancellations 48+ hours ahead are free. Late cancels or no-shows are charged the full lesson fee. Thanks for understanding!

## 3. Lenient (12h / 50% kept)
**Policy:** Free cancellation up to 12 hours before. 50% charged for late cancels or no-shows.
**Parent message:**
> A card on file is required to book. Cancel 12+ hours ahead for free. Late cancels or no-shows are charged 50% of the lesson. Thanks!

## 4. First Lesson Free
**Policy:** First lesson is free (no charge). After that, standard 24h/50% policy applies.
**Parent message:**
> Your first lesson is free — no card needed! After that, I require a card on file: free cancel 24h ahead, 50% kept for late cancels/no-shows. Looking forward to meeting you!

## 5. No-Show Only (24h / 100% no-show, free late cancel)
**Policy:** Late cancels (within 24h) are free. No-shows charged 100%.
**Parent message:**
> Card on file required. Late cancels (within 24h) are free to reschedule. No-shows are charged the full lesson fee. Thanks for your understanding!

## 6. 24h / 100% kept (strict standard)
**Policy:** Free cancel 24h ahead. Full fee charged for late cancels or no-shows.
**Parent message:**
> To reserve your slot, a card is required. Cancel 24+ hours ahead for free. Late cancels or no-shows are charged the full lesson fee. Thanks!

## 7. Rolling (no fixed window — 24h / 50%, with 1 free pass per month)
**Policy:** 24h/50% standard, but each student gets 1 free late-cancel per month.
**Parent message:**
> Card on file required. Free cancel 24h ahead. Late cancels/no-shows charged 50%, but you get 1 free pass per month. Thanks!

## 8. Holiday/Summer (48h / 50%)
**Policy:** For peak seasons — 48h window, 50% kept.
**Parent message:**
> During busy season, a card on file is required. Cancel 48+ hours ahead for free. Late cancels/no-shows charged 50%. Thanks for helping me plan!

## 9. Trial Student (first 3 lessons, 12h / 25%)
**Policy:** For new/trial students — shorter window, lower kept fee.
**Parent message:**
> Welcome! For your first 3 trial lessons, card on file required: free cancel 12h ahead, 25% kept for late cancels/no-shows. After that, standard policy applies.

## 10. Flexible (tutor discretion — 24h / 50% with forgiving button)
**Policy:** Standard 24h/50%, but tutor can 1-click forgive any charge.
**Parent message:**
> Card on file required. Free cancel 24h ahead; late cancels/no-shows charged 50%. I can forgive charges anytime if life happens — just let me know. Thanks!
