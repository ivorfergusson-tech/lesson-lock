# LessonLock — Marketing Plan (30-Day Short-Form Video System)

**Product:** LessonLock — after-booking protection for solo music tutors (US/UK/CA/AU). 2-min no-show policy: students put a card on file once, tutor auto-charges kept fees on no-show/late cancel.
**ICP:** Maya, 37, online piano teacher, 22 students, $45/hr, Zoom, hates awkward texts. 10-40 students, $30-80/hr.
**Pricing:** Starter $12/mo (40 protected bookings, email) · Pro $29/mo (unlimited, SMS, custom logo, 5% of kept fees capped $40).
**Positioning (1 sentence):** The 2-minute no-show policy for online music teachers — students put a card on file once, you get paid automatically when they flake.
**Channels (organic only):** TikTok, IG Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn. FB groups + LinkedIn written posts are the warm-up funnel (see marketing-campaign.md). Goal: 5 paid $12 pre-auth by Day 30, ≥60% card capture.

> Note: live web search was unavailable (HTTP 402) during this run. Competitor/angle research below is drawn from the product docs (Fresha, Calendly+Stripe, Stripe Payment Links, HoneyBook, Preply/Superprof) plus general short-form best practice. Refresh with live search before filming.

## 1. Deep research (summary)

**Competitors & what works**
- Fresha (salon/no-show protection): proves non-salon pros pay for no-show protection. Angle to steal: "protected revenue" framing + monthly statement.
- Calendly + Stripe Payment Links: manual, no policy enforcement → our gap: automated policy + reminders + evidence log.
- HoneyBook / Preply / Superprof: marketplace/CRM, no auto kept-fee charge. Angle: we're a protection layer, not a marketplace.
- No-show policy templates: high save/share rate on TikTok ("copy this text" videos).

**Trending hooks/formats that work for tutors**
- "I made $X from no-shows last month" + Stripe screenshot (proof-led).
- "Copy this exact message to parents" (template-led, high saves).
- "3 mistakes music teachers make with cancellations" (listicle).
- "Day X building..." (build-in-public, LinkedIn + TikTok).
- POV: "student no-shows, I still get paid" (relatable, shareable).

**Content pillars (5)**
1. Pain & money — lost income, awkward chasing (relatability).
2. Solution & how-to — policy templates, auto-charge walkthrough (utility).
3. Proof & recovery — "$ recovered" screenshots, testimonials (trust).
4. Build in public — journey, metrics, lessons (LinkedIn + niche creators).
5. Free value — templates, scripts, stats (lead gen → email capture).

**Brand voice:** Warm, practical, tutor-to-tutor, slightly cheeky about "getting paid when they flake." No corporate jargon. Evidence-led (numbers, screenshots). Empathetic to awkwardness.

## 4. Monetization layer

Funnel: Clip → link in bio / landing (lessonlock.app) → email capture → interview/pilot → $12 pre-auth (Starter month 1) → activation (first protected booking with card saved) → Pro upsell ($29 + success fee).

Email sequence (3 emails over 7 days):
1. Day 0: free template + "here's how tutors use it" (value).
2. Day 3: case study "$ recovered" + CTA try LessonLock.
3. Day 7: "10 spots early access" + kill-gate urgency.

**3 conversion experiments (Month 1)**
- A: CTA "Comment POLICY" vs "Link in bio" → measure DM-to-email rate.
- B: Hook "I made $X" vs "Copy this message" → measure save rate + profile visits.
- C: Landing page with vs without "1 kept lesson pays for 4 months" anchor → measure email capture %.

## 5. Measurement dashboard

Weekly (per platform): posts, impressions/views, watch time (avg %), saves, shares, comments, profile visits, link clicks, DMs, interviews booked, email captures, pilot signups, $12 pre-auths, card-capture rate, protected bookings, $ recovered.

Kill gate Day 30: ≥5 paid $12 pre-auth · ≥60% card capture · ≥20 protected bookings · ≥$1k recovered · <8% churn · 0 chargeback losses.
