LESSONLOCK — MARKETING DIGEST (30-DAY PLAN)
===========================================

PRODUCT: LessonLock — no-show protection for online music teachers. Students put a card on file once; tutor auto-charges kept fees on no-show/late cancel. 2-min setup.
ICP: Maya, 37, online piano teacher, 22 students, $45/hr, Zoom. 10-40 students, $30-80/hr, US/UK/CA/AU.
PRICING: Starter $12/mo (40 protected bookings, email) | Pro $29/mo (unlimited, SMS, custom logo, 5% of kept fees capped $40).
POSITIONING: The 2-minute no-show policy for online music teachers — students put a card on file once, you get paid automatically when they flake.
CHANNELS (organic): TikTok, IG Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn. FB groups + LinkedIn posts warm the funnel.
GOAL: 5 paid $12 pre-auth by Day 30, >=60% card capture.

PILLARS: 1 Pain & money  2 Solution & how-to  3 Proof & recovery  4 Build in public  5 Free value
VOICE: warm, tutor-to-tutor, cheeky about "getting paid when they flake", evidence-led, empathetic.

HOOK BANK (sample): "I made $847 from no-shows last month." / "Copy this exact message to parents." / "POV: your student no-shows and you STILL get paid." / "Stop eating the cost of no-shows." / "The 2-minute policy that stopped my students from flaking."

HASHTAGS (core): #musiclessons #pianoteacher #guitarteacher #voiceteacher #drumteacher #noshowpolicy #musicteacherlife #onlinelessons #lessonlock

CALENDAR: 16 clips over 30 days, 3-5/week. Each clip: title/platform/pillar/goal, 3s hook + on-screen text, timestamped script with b-roll cues, shot list, captions + 8-15 hashtags, posting time + engagement prompt, video-generator prompt (9:16), success metric.
POSTING TIMES: TikTok/Reels 7-9pm local; Shorts 12-2pm; LinkedIn 8-10am Tue-Thu.

MONETIZATION FUNNEL: Clip -> link in bio/landing -> email capture -> interview/pilot -> $12 pre-auth -> activation (first protected booking with card saved) -> Pro upsell.
3 EXPERIMENTS: (A) CTA "Comment POLICY" vs "Link in bio"; (B) hook "$X" vs "Copy this message"; (C) landing anchor "1 kept lesson pays for 4 months" on/off.

KILL GATE DAY 30: >=5 paid $12 pre-auth | >=60% card capture | >=20 protected bookings | >=$1k recovered | <8% churn | 0 chargeback losses.

TOP 3 TO FILM FIRST (this week):
1. "I lost $X to no-shows" — pain, high relatability, drives comments.
2. "Copy this exact message" — utility, high saves, lead gen.
3. "POV: student no-shows, you still get paid" — proof concept, shareable.
