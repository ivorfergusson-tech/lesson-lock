# LessonLock — Marketing Campaign (LinkedIn + Facebook)

**Goal:** Drive 30 teacher interviews + 10 concierge pilot signups in 30 days. Value-first, not spammy.

---

## Platform strategy

### Facebook (primary — community groups)
- **Groups:** "Piano Teachers" (150k+), "Guitar Teachers Forum", "Voice Teachers Network", "Drum Teachers"
- **Approach:** Value-first posts. Share a free resource (policy template, no-show stats) → soft CTA to interview/early access.
- **Frequency:** 2-3 posts/week per group. Engage in comments daily.
- **Rules:** Read group rules. Never spam links. Always lead with value.

### LinkedIn (secondary — professional credibility)
- **Audience:** Music teachers, music school owners, EdTech founders, indie SaaS builders.
- **Approach:** Build-in-public posts. Share journey, metrics, lessons learned. Tag relevant people.
- **Frequency:** 3 posts/week. Comment on others' posts daily.

---

## Content pillars

1. **Pain point** — "I lost $X to no-shows this month"
2. **Solution** — "How I automated no-show charges (no awkward texts)"
3. **Social proof** — "Teacher recovered $X with policy X"
4. **Build in public** — "Day X of building LessonLock"
5. **Free value** — policy templates, no-show stats, scripts

---

## Week-by-week campaign

### Week 1 — Seed & Listen
- [ ] Join 5 relevant FB groups + engage (comment on 5 posts/day, no links)
- [ ] Post intro in 2 groups: "I'm researching no-shows for music teachers — would love your input" (link to interview)
- [ ] LinkedIn: "I'm building a tool for music teachers to handle no-shows. Here's what I've learned so far." (build-in-public)
- [ ] DM 10 teachers from groups with the interview outreach message
- [ ] Share free policy template #1 in 1 group (value-first, no CTA)

### Week 2 — Value & Interviews
- [ ] Post "5 no-show policy templates for music teachers" in 3 groups (link to landing page)
- [ ] LinkedIn: "3 things piano teachers told me about no-shows" (insights from interviews)
- [ ] Share a real stat: "Average music teacher loses $X/month to no-shows" (with source)
- [ ] DM 10 more teachers
- [ ] Reply to every comment on your posts within 2 hours

### Week 3 — Social proof & Pilots
- [ ] LinkedIn: "I just signed up my first 3 pilot tutors. Here's what happened." (build-in-public)
- [ ] FB: Share a pilot testimonial (with permission): "I recovered $X from a no-show"
- [ ] Post "Ready-to-send parent message for no-show charges" (free template)
- [ ] DM teachers who engaged with previous posts
- [ ] Start 10 concierge pilots (manual onboarding)

### Week 4 — Conversion & Launch prep
- [ ] LinkedIn: "Day 30 update: X interviews, X pilots, X card captures" (transparency)
- [ ] FB: "Early access: 10 spots for music teachers" (scarcity, link to landing page)
- [ ] Share Stripe screenshot (with permission): "I made $X from no-show fees"
- [ ] Collect 3 "I recovered $X" quotes for landing page
- [ ] Evaluate kill gate: ≥5 paid pre-auth? ≥60% card capture?

## Engagement rules
- Reply to every comment within 2 hours (first 48h after posting)
- Ask follow-up questions in comments to drive discussion
- DM anyone who engages meaningfully — offer 1-on-1 chat
- Never delete negative comments — address them honestly
- Track: posts, impressions, comments, DMs, interviews booked

## Kill gate (Day 30)
- [ ] ≥5 paid $12 pre-auth?
- [ ] ≥60% card capture?
- [ ] ≥20 protected bookings?
- [ ] ≥$1k student fees recovered?
- [ ] <8% monthly churn?
- [ ] 0 chargeback losses?

If NO → kill or pivot to general solo coaches with same tech.
If YES → proceed to Phase 3 (convert & publish).
